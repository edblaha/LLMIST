## Personal Data
To understand what we do with Personal Data, it is important to start with how we define it:
> Personal Data (sometimes referred to as Personal Information) is information that identifies, relates to, describes, is capable of being associated with, or could reasonably link - directly or indirectly - to an individual.
So, what does this really mean? Personal Data is more than your name and email address - it includes other “identifiers” connected to you. All of this information comes together so we have a better understanding of you as a weather enthusiast, along with your interests and preferences, so we can personalize your experience.

## Data Collection Technologies and Consumer Choice
We strive to provide you with a personalized experience. Here's how we tailor our Services to you:

#### Data Collection Technologies
In addition to the data you provide directly to us (as outlined in the above sections), we and our partners use various technologies to track usage and personalize your experience. These include:
- Cookies: Small text files stored on your device to remember preferences and track usage across different websites.
- ETags: Labels that websites use to identify when your web browser has accessed a page before, which help websites track if you've visited a page previously and what information you've already shared.
- JavaScript: Programming language that websites use to collect information about how you interact with them, which allows websites to track your clicks, scrolls, and other actions.
- Local storage: Browser or device storage, saves user preferences and other data for a smoother experience.
- SDKs: Toolkits that apps and software use to collect data about how you use them. They provide developers with pre-made code and tools to gather information about your activity.
- Web beacons: Tiny graphic images used to understand how you interact with content.
These tools (which we’ll refer to, collectively, as “cookies and trackers”) help us understand your interactions with our Services and track you across different websites for various purposes, such as cross-contextual behavioral advertising (CCBA), commonly referred to as “targeted advertising.”
Please note that cookies and trackers may be used within our marketing and newsletter emails to track your interactions with our Services and when emails are opened.

#### Your Cookie and Tracker Choices
Depending on your location, you may have control over non-essential tracking technologies used on our websites through the Services (for example, in the EU/EEA/UK). You can also visit your browser’s settings to see what you can control from there. We categorize our cookies and trackers as follows:
- Essential Technology cookies/trackers allow access to information stored in your browser needed to perform necessary functions - including securing the connection to our websites, inferring approximate location data of your internet connection to provide you weather forecasts for your general area, and optimizing site performance.
- Functional Technology cookies/trackers allow permission for our websites to remember choices you make and collect information about how you use the Services in order to improve our weather offerings and business. They enable us to provide you with the most accurate forecasts while also measuring performance, and improving our weather and forecast products and our Services. Additionally, they allow for social media platforms to collect data needed to display editorial articles and videos on our Services.
- Targeted Advertising cookies/trackers allow our advertising partners to deliver ads and content personalized to your interests, resulting in a more relevant browsing experience. We learn about your interests from your activity on our websites, as well as from other websites you use.
- Geographically Relevant Advertising cookies/trackers enable the use and sharing of location information embedded within your IP address for advertising purposes. This enables our advertising vendors to show you ads related to your current location, creating a more relevant browsing experience.
See section “General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Notice” for information about data processing purposes and legal basis.

#### Advertising Vendors
We work with Advertising Vendors and our advertisers (including their vendors) to serve relevant ads and optimize our Services. We and our Advertising Vendors may use the following techniques:
- Advertising identifiers: Collecting information about usage of the mobile apps through advertising identifiers to support or deliver personalized advertising. An advertising identifier is a randomly-generated alphanumeric code associated with your device. Advertising identifiers provide advertisers a way to identify your device without using a permanent device identifier, such as serial number. On a website, cookie IDs can be used in this same manner.
- Combining data: Merging information from various sources to better understand your interests. This includes ingesting data about you from external sources and using identity graphs to match various identifiers (including hashed email addresses, defined below).
- Cross-device tracking: Tracking your activity across your other websites or devices to provide a consistent experience.
- Hashed email addresses: Converting email addresses into an irreversible string of characters (hashes) that allow for ad targeting and measurement across platforms without exposing your actual email address.

#### Your Advertising Choices
You have choices over how your Personal Data is used for advertising:
1. Browser settings: We encourage you to visit the help or settings area of your browser to manage cookies and local storage.
2. Device settings: You can control advertising identifiers on mobile devices through device/operating system settings.
3. Industry opt-outs: Use tools provided by the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA), Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada (DAAC), and European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA) to opt out of personalized ads from multiple vendors.
4. Opt-outs: Depending on your location, you may have opt-outs available through the Services.
5. Opt-out signals: We will honor GPC (Global Privacy Control) signals in the U.S. when received from your browser. To learn more about Global Privacy Control, you can visit https://globalprivacycontrol.org. As Do Not Track is not a universally-recognized browser signal, we do not recognize this on our consumer weather products.
6. Vendors: You can visit our Data Vendors page to review the vendors we share data with and view their privacy policy and opt-out mechanisms where applicable. In some countries, you may also be asked to review and make choices about Vendor activity prior to accessing our Services.
Some things to remember:
- If you change your settings on individual web browsers or ad networks, these settings will not necessarily carry over to other browsers or ad networks, and you may still see our ads.
- Opting out doesn't mean you'll stop seeing ads – they just might not be as relevant to you. Some ads are displayed based on the content you view. This is often referred to as “contextual advertising.” As these ads are not targeted to you individually, your opt-out choice will not apply to them.
