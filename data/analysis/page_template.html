Rank

Read the scores with care. A longer, more detailed policy can score higher simply because it admits more. A short policy that says little can look better than it is. The scores rate what each policy says the company may do, not what it does.

Most of these policies score high. The clearest result is the gap between the ad-supported platforms and Wikipedia and DuckDuckGo. The order within the top group is close and depends on the weights.

How each criterion was scored
    {{RUBRIC}}

If a policy said nothing at all about a criterion, it would score 1 and be marked “not stated”. A clear “we don't do this” scores 0. Every policy addressed every criterion at least in part. Where part of a criterion was missing, such as AI training, the score uses what the policy does say, and the detail lists what's missing.

How we did this

  • Collecting. On October 21, 2026 we downloaded each platform's official privacy policy and saved the original page and a plain-text copy. Meta and Reddit blocked automated downloads, so we used copies saved from a web browser. OpenAI also blocked us, and we left it out. ESPN uses Disney's privacy policy.
  • Reading. We pulled out {{N_FIND}} findings about what each policy says on collection, use, sharing, keeping and control, each with an exact quote and its line number in the saved text.
  • Checking the words. A script confirms that every quote appears word for word in the saved policy. Every quote on this page passed, as did every quote in the full set of findings.
  • Checking the meaning. A matching quote can still be misread, so we re-read {{N_REV}} findings against the surrounding paragraph. {{N_FIXED}} claims said more than the policy did and were reworded. For the three most striking findings (Meta, TikTok and Weather.com), we also went back to the original saved web page.
  • Limits. These are the English-language versions shown to US visitors. Several policies send details to separate pages, such as state-law notices, cookie policies and AI notices, which we didn't analyze. Policies change; these are the versions from October 21, 2026.
