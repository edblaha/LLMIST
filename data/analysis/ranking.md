# Platform ranking: how aggressive is each privacy policy?

Generated by `data/analysis/build_scores.py` from `data/analysis/scoring_spec.json`. Scores are 0-3 per criterion; weighted total is 0-100. Evidence quotes are in `data/analysis/scores.json`.

## Criteria and weights

- **Collects data you never gave it** (30%): 0 = Essentially only what you provide; says it builds no profile; 1 = Automatic device/usage data; any partner or location data is limited or opt-in; no inferred profile; 2 = Builds inferences about you OR receives data about you from other companies; 3 = Inferences AND data from other companies AND precise location or biometrics
- **Uses it for ads or AI training** (25%): 0 = Neither personalized ads nor AI training; 1 = Only contextual (non-personal) ads; 2 = Personalized ads OR AI training on user data; 3 = Personalized ads AND AI training on user data
- **Shares or sells it** (20%): 0 = Only with service providers / for legal demands; 1 = Also limited partners under privacy restrictions; 2 = Identifiers or personal data go to advertising partners or affiliates; 3 = Admits a 'sale'/'sharing' under state law, licenses user content, or lets third parties use it for their own purposes
- **Hard to opt out** (15%): 0 = Nothing to opt out of, or one clear setting; 1 = A few clear steps; 2 = 4+ separate places, or opt-outs only linked, not explained; 3 = As 2, AND a major use (on-platform ads or AI training) with no opt-out described
- **Keeps it a long time** (10%): 0 = Specific short limits; 1 = Specific limits for the main data; 2 = 'As long as needed' / for the life of the account, no fixed limit; 3 = Your content stays after you delete your account, or the account can't be deleted

Not-stated rule: A criterion the policy says nothing about scores 1 and is flagged 'not stated'. A clear statement that the company does NOT do something scores 0. Where a policy addresses part of a criterion but is silent on another part (e.g. ads stated, AI training not stated), the score is based on what is stated and the silent part is listed in 'not_stated_parts'.
